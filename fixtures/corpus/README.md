# Qualification Corpus Governance

No qualification corpus is included at Task 1 / M0.

Before a corpus item can be used, its local record must include immutable origin, checksum, license, redistribution terms, consent when applicable, and authorization for the intended processing. Adult voices, child speech, transcriptions, music, and other identifiable or copyrighted material require separate evidence appropriate to their source. The corpus remains local and is not uploaded by the product.

Corpus records do not approve the model asset, and model-asset approval does not approve corpus material. Both remain separate release gates.
